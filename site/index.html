The working record

A history made for documents, not developers.

You shouldn’t need Git commands—or a storage subscription—to answer a simple question: what happened to this file?

  1. 01

    Watch the folder you already use

    Keep Word, OpenDocument, PDF, and text files where they are. The app only reads originals and saves content-addressed snapshots in its private local archive.

  2. 02

    Read the change sheet

    Choose any date to compare extracted paragraphs and table cells. Additions are underlined; removals are struck through, like familiar proof marks.

  3. 03

    Restore without a leap of faith

    Before restoring, the current file gets one last safety snapshot. Then the exact earlier bytes—not a converted copy—return to their original path.
